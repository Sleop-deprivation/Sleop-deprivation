About Me

A Bit About Me

I'm a very chill and down-to-earth person. I get along well with many people, but can work by myself just as well. I also push myself to my limits whenever possible, and if there's something I need to know for a project but don't, I will know it by the end.

Other Interests

I'm not just a coder. I've played piano since I was a toddler, I know how to make pixel art, and I play games, both video and board. Some personal favorites include the video game Octopath Traveler and the board game Betrayal at House on the Hill. I also enjoy playing TTRPGs when I get the chance.

Me and Coding

I started coding in 6th grade when I discovered Scratch, and have loved it ever since. I moved on to coding my TI-84 for the rest of middle school, then went to Brooklyn Technical High School and eventually Rochester Institute of Technology to further hone my coding skills to and beyond their limits.
